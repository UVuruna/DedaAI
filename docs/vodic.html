Deda — tvoj besplatan ključ

Da bi Deda razgovarao, potreban mu je tvoj lični Google ključ. Besplatan je, ne traži karticu, i pravi se za dva minuta. Idi redom kroz slike.

Zašto svoj ključ?

Svako koristi svoj ključ — tako niko ne troši tuđu besplatnu kvotu i Deda uvek radi. Ključ je kao lozinka: čuvaj ga i ne deli ga.

1

Otvori stranicu

U pregledaču (na telefonu ili računaru) ukucaj adresu aistudio.google.com/apikey i pritisni enter.

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Ukucaj adresu u pregledač:
aistudio.google.com/apikey
Radi na telefonu i na računaru — u bilo kom pregledaču.
2

Prijavi se na Google

Ako te pita, prijavi se svojim Google (Gmail) nalogom: ukucaj mejl pa Next, zatim lozinku. Ako si već prijavljen, ovaj korak preskačeš.

3

Prihvati uslove

Ako se pojave uslovi korišćenja, štikliraj kvadratiće i klikni Continue. (Ne pojave se svima.)

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Google AI Studio

✔ I agree to the additional terms.
Štikliraj kvadratiće pa Continue.

Potvrdi

Deda — your free key

For Deda to talk, it needs your own Google key. It is free, needs no credit card, and takes two minutes. Just follow the pictures in order.

Why your own key?

Everyone uses their own key — so no one drains anyone else’s free quota and Deda always works. The key is like a password: keep it, don’t share it.

1

Open the page

In a browser (phone or computer) type the address aistudio.google.com/apikey and press enter.

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Type the address in a browser:
aistudio.google.com/apikey
Works on phone and computer — in any browser.
2

Sign in to Google

If asked, sign in with your Google (Gmail) account: type your email then Next, then your password. If already signed in, skip this step.

3

Accept the terms

If terms of service appear, tick the boxes and click Continue. (Not shown to everyone.)

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Google AI Studio
Terms of Service
✔ I accept the Google APIs Terms of Service.
✔ I agree to the additional terms.
Tick the boxes then Continue.
4

Create the key

Click the blue Create API key button.

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Google AI Studio
API keys
You have no keys yet.
Click the blue Create API key button.
5

Confirm

Click Create API key in new project. If it asks for a project, keep the suggested one.

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Google AI Studio
Create API key
Search Google Cloud projects
My first project ▾
Click Create API key in new project.
6

Copy the key

Your key appears (it starts with AIza…). Click Copy to copy it.

aistudio.google.com/apikey
Google AI Studio
API key generated
AIzaSy••••••••••••••••••••
Save this key. Do not share it.
Click Copy — the key is copied.
7

Paste into Deda

Open the Deda app, tap the gear (Settings) top-right. Long-press the API Key field and choose Paste. Go back (arrow or swipe) — the key is saved.

Done!

Say “Hej Deda” and ask it something. If it says it has no key, go back to step 7 and check you pasted the whole key.
